Hello! I'm Lennart, a Computer Engineering doctorate student currently based in Tokyo, Japan. Welcome to my personal website where you can learn more about my work, research, and passions.

## About Me

I have a passion for computers, their architecture, software and more. 

I started my journey in Computer Engineering at Leibniz University Hannover, Germany. Currently I am a doctorate student at Kise Laboratory of the Department of Computer Science at the Institute of Science Tokyo. My research focuses on Computer Architecture, with a particular interest in RTL simulation acceleration, Verilog HDL compilation, RISC-V, and FPGA.

When I'm not studying, I enjoy studying Japanese, playing video games and watching anime and movies.

## Research Interests

- **Computer Architecture** — processor design and hardware optimization
- **RTL Simulation** — accelerating simulation of digital circuits through compiler techniques
- **RISC-V** — open-source instruction set architecture and CPU simulation
- **FPGA** — reconfigurable computing and hardware prototyping
- **Verilog HDL** — hardware description language compilation and tooling
