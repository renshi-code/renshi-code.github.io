*To appear in the IEEE International Symposium on Embedded Multicore/Many-core Systems-on-Chip (MCSoC 2026), December 2026.*

This work investigates the fidelity of cycle-accurate software simulation for RISC-V processors extended with domain-specific Custom Function Units (CFU). We demonstrate rigorous behavioral parity between synthesizable Verilog RTL simulated with Verilator and a high-performance C++ 5-stage coroutine pipeline simulator (SimRV), enabling rapid architectural exploration without sacrificing hardware accuracy.
