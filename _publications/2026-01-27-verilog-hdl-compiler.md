This paper presents the design and implementation of a Verilog HDL compiler (referred to as the VCC compiler) aimed at accelerating the simulation of RTL circuits developed using Verilog HDL. The work was conducted at the Kise Laboratory, Department of Computer Science, Institute of Science Tokyo.
