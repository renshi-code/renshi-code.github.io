## Overview

**VCC** (Verilog Circuit Compiler) is a specialized hardware description language compiler developed at the [Kise Laboratory (ArchLab)](https://www.arch.cs.titech.ac.jp), Department of Computer Science, Institute of Science Tokyo. Its core mission is to overcome traditional bottlenecks in Register-Transfer Level (RTL) simulation of complex digital circuits.

### Key Highlights & Technical Features

- **RTL Simulation Acceleration:** Translates Verilog HDL designs into optimized executable code, achieving significant execution speedup over standard event-driven interpretation.
- **Compiler Optimizations:** Employs static analysis, signal dependency scheduling, and parallel execution optimizations specifically tuned for hardware simulation workloads.
- **Hardware Evaluation:** Benchmark evaluations conducted across real-world digital circuits and RISC-V processor models.
- **Publication & Precedents:** Published and presented at the IEICE Technical Committee on VLSI Design Technologies (VLD) / Reconfigurable Systems (RECONF).
